Frequently Asked Questions

What is a text case converter?

A text case converter is an online tool that changes text capitalization between formats such as uppercase, lowercase, Title Case and Sentence case.

How do I convert text to uppercase?

Paste your text into the converter and click UPPERCASE. The text will immediately be converted to capital letters.

How do I change uppercase text to lowercase?

Paste your text into the converter and click lowercase. All letters will be changed to lowercase.

What is Title Case?

Title Case is a capitalization format commonly used for headings and titles where words are formatted with initial capital letters.

What is Sentence Case?

Sentence case uses normal sentence capitalization, with the first letter of a sentence capitalized and the remaining text normally formatted in lowercase.
