What Is a Meeting Notes Generator?

A meeting notes generator is an online tool that helps organize information from meetings into a consistent structure. Meeting notes can capture discussion points, decisions, action items, responsibilities and follow-up activities.

Meeting Notes for Teams and Businesses

Clear meeting documentation can help teams keep track of what was discussed and what needs to happen next. Managers, project teams and business professionals can use structured meeting notes to keep important information easier to review.

Meeting Notes for Project Management

Project meetings often include decisions, responsibilities and deadlines. Organizing these details into structured notes can make it easier for project teams to review agreed actions after a meeting.

Meeting Notes for Managers

Managers can use meeting notes to record important discussions, decisions and follow-up actions from team meetings, one-to-one meetings and business discussions.

Meeting Notes for Consultants

Consultants can use structured meeting notes to record client discussions, important decisions, requirements and next steps after meetings.

Meeting Action Items and Follow-Ups

Action items are an important part of effective meeting documentation. Recording next steps and owners helps create a clear reference for tasks that need to be followed up after the meeting.

Meeting Minutes Generator vs. Meeting Notes

Meeting notes and meeting minutes both document information from a meeting, but they can serve different purposes. Meeting notes are often used as practical working records, while formal meeting minutes may be prepared as an official record of a meeting.
