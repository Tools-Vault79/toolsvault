How to Calculate ROI

To calculate return on investment, first determine the total investment cost by adding the initial investment and any additional costs. Then subtract the total investment cost from the total return to determine the net gain.

ROI = (Net Gain ÷ Total Investment Cost) × 100

For example, if an investment costs $1,100 in total and produces a return of $1,500, the net gain is $400. The ROI is approximately 36.36%.

What Is Return on Investment?

Return on Investment, commonly known as ROI, is a percentage used to express the gain or loss generated by an investment compared with the amount invested.

ROI can be used when reviewing investments, marketing campaigns, business projects, equipment purchases and other activities where costs and financial returns can be compared.

Frequently Asked Questions About ROI

What is an ROI calculator?

An ROI calculator calculates return on investment by comparing the net gain from an investment with the total investment cost.

How do you calculate ROI?

ROI is commonly calculated by subtracting the total investment cost from the return to determine net gain, dividing the net gain by total investment cost and multiplying the result by 100.

What does ROI percentage mean?

ROI percentage expresses the net gain from an investment as a percentage of the total investment cost.

What is return multiple?

Return multiple compares the total return with the total investment cost. For example, a 2x return multiple means the return is twice the total investment cost.
