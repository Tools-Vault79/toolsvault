How to Create a Business Quote

Enter your business name and client name, add the product or service you are quoting, specify the quantity and unit price, and include your payment or quotation terms. The tool then creates an organized quotation that you can review and copy.

What Is a Business Quote?

A business quote, also called a quotation or sales quote, outlines the products or services offered to a customer along with pricing, quantities and relevant terms. A clear quotation helps customers understand what is being offered before they make a purchasing decision.
