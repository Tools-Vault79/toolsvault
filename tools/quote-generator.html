How to Create a Professional Business Quote

  1. Enter your business name.
  2. Add the customer or client name.
  3. Enter a quote number and quotation date.

  5. Enter the quantity and unit price.

  7. Set the quotation validity period.
  8. Add payment and quotation terms.

What Should a Business Quote Include?

A professional business quote commonly contains the business name, customer details, quote reference number, date, validity period, products or services, quantities, unit prices, applicable taxes, discounts and payment terms. These elements help the customer understand the proposed price and scope before agreeing to the work.

  • Business or company name
  • Customer or client name
  • Quotation number
  • Quotation date
  • Quote validity date
  • Products or services
  • Quantity and unit price
  • Discounts and taxes where applicable
  • Payment and quotation terms

Quote Generator vs Invoice Generator

A quote generally presents proposed pricing before a customer agrees to purchase a product or service. An invoice is generally used to request payment for goods or services that have been supplied or agreed for billing.

If you need to request payment rather than provide proposed pricing, use an invoice generator instead.
