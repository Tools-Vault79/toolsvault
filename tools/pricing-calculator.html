Profit Margin and Markup

Profit margin and markup are related but different pricing concepts. Profit margin is generally expressed as profit as a percentage of the selling price, while markup is generally calculated in relation to cost.

Understanding the difference can help businesses review their pricing approach and compare different selling prices.
