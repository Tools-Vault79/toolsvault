Pricing a product correctly requires more than adding a markup to its cost. Payment processing fees, marketplace charges, operating expenses and your target profit margin can all affect the final selling price.

How to Calculate a Selling Price

Start with the total cost of producing or providing the product or service. Add additional expenses, account for payment or marketplace fees, and apply your desired profit margin to estimate the selling price.

Pricing Calculator Formula

Estimated Selling Price = Total Cost ÷ (1 − Fee Rate − Profit Margin Rate)

For example, if the total cost is $22, the fee is 5%, and the target profit margin is 30%, the estimated price is calculated using those values. The calculator helps review the relationship between costs, fees and the desired margin.

Profit Margin and Markup

Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of the selling price, while markup is generally calculated in relation to product or service cost. Understanding the difference helps businesses compare pricing approaches.
