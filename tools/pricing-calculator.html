What Is a Pricing Calculator?

A pricing calculator is an online tool that helps businesses estimate how much to charge for products or services. It can combine costs, fees and target profit margins to produce a suggested selling price.

A pricing calculator can be useful when launching a product, reviewing existing prices, comparing costs, or creating a pricing strategy for an online business.

Pricing Calculator for Small Businesses

Small businesses, freelancers, agencies, online sellers and service providers can use a business pricing calculator to estimate costs, selling prices and potential profit margins.

For businesses selling products online, accounting for payment processing fees, marketplace fees and other costs can help create a more realistic pricing estimate.

Product Pricing Calculator and Pricing Estimator

A product pricing calculator can help you work backwards from your costs and desired profit margin to estimate a suitable selling price. This can be useful for physical products, digital products, services and other business offers.

Profit Margin and Markup Calculations

Profit margin and markup are related but different pricing concepts. A pricing tool can help you review the relationship between your costs, selling price and expected profit. For more detailed calculations, you can also use the ToolVault Profit Calculator.
