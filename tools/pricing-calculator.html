How to Calculate a Selling Price

To calculate a selling price, start with the total cost of producing or providing the product or service. Add relevant additional expenses and consider payment processing or marketplace fees. You can then work backward from your desired profit margin to estimate the selling price required to achieve your target.

ToolVault's pricing calculator uses your entered costs, fee percentage and desired profit margin to estimate the selling price required to cover those costs while accounting for the target margin.

Pricing Calculator Formula

Estimated Selling Price Total Cost ÷ (1 − Fee % − Profit Margin %)

For example, if your total cost is $22 and you enter a payment or platform fee of 5% with a desired profit margin of 30%, the calculator uses these values to estimate the required selling price.

Profit Margin and Markup

Profit margin and markup are related but different pricing concepts. Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of the selling price, while markup is generally calculated in relation to the cost of a product or service.

Understanding the difference between margin and markup can help businesses evaluate different approaches to product and service pricing.
