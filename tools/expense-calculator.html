Use the calculator to enter your expected monthly budget and the main expenses associated with running your business.

  • Enter your monthly business budget.
  • Add your rent or office expenses.
  • Enter utility costs.

  • Add other recurring expenses.
  • Calculate the expense summary.

What Does the Expense Calculator Calculate?

Total Expenses

The total expenses figure combines the expense amounts entered into the calculator. This provides a quick view of your planned monthly spending.

Remaining Budget

The remaining budget compares your monthly budget with the total expenses entered. This can help you see how much of the planned budget remains after the listed costs.

Expense Ratio

The expense ratio shows total expenses as a percentage of the monthly budget. It provides another way to review spending against the budget entered into the calculator.

Largest Expense

The largest expense identifies which of the listed expense categories has the highest value.
