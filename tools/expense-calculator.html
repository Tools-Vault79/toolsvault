What Does the Expense Calculator Calculate?

Total Expenses

Total expenses are calculated by adding all expense amounts entered into the calculator. This gives you a quick view of your planned monthly spending.

Remaining Budget

Remaining budget is calculated by subtracting total listed expenses from the monthly budget. A positive result indicates that part of the entered budget remains after the listed expenses.

Expense Ratio

The expense ratio shows total expenses as a percentage of the monthly budget entered into the calculator. It provides another way to compare planned expenses with the available budget.

Largest Expense

The largest expense identifies the highest-value category among the expense amounts entered into the calculator.
