What Is a Social Media Content Planner?

A social media content planner is a tool that helps organize ideas for social media posts before they are published. A planner can help businesses and creators structure their content around topics, audiences, platforms and publishing periods.

Social Media Planner for Small Businesses

Small businesses can use a weekly social media planner to organize educational posts, promotional content, customer questions, product information, tips and other content ideas. Planning content in advance can make the weekly publishing process more organized.

Weekly Social Media Content Plan

A weekly social media content plan provides a simple framework for organizing seven days of content. Instead of deciding what to post every day, you can start with a structured set of content ideas based on your audience and topic.

Social Media Planner vs Social Media Scheduler

A social media planner helps organize content ideas and publishing plans. A social media scheduler is a separate type of tool that can connect to social networks and schedule posts for publication. ToolVault's Social Media Planner focuses on planning and generating content ideas rather than automatically publishing posts.
