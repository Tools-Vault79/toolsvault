How to Use the Invoice Generator

Creating an invoice with ToolVault is straightforward. Enter the required information and add the products or services you want to include.

  • Enter your business name and invoice number.
  • Select the invoice date and payment due date.
  • Add your business address.
  • Enter the client's billing information.
  • Add products or services as invoice items.
  • Enter quantities and unit prices.
  • Add tax or a discount when applicable.

  • Update and review the generated invoice.

What Is an Invoice Generator?

An invoice generator is an online tool that helps create invoices for products and services. Instead of manually calculating every line item, an invoice maker can automatically calculate amounts and organize important billing information into a professional invoice format.

Invoice Generator for Small Business

Small businesses can use an online invoice generator to prepare invoices for customers and clients. Common users include consultants, freelancers, agencies, contractors, service providers and other businesses that bill for products or services.

Business Invoice Generator

A business invoice should clearly identify the seller, customer, invoice number, dates, items, prices and total amount due. ToolVault's invoice generator provides fields for these core details in one place.

Invoice Generator for Freelancers

Invoice Maker With Automatic Calculations
