How to Calculate Business Profit

Business profit is generally calculated by subtracting total costs from total revenue.

Net Profit = Total Revenue − Total Costs

Profit Margin Calculator

Profit margin shows the percentage of revenue that remains after costs are deducted.

Profit Margin = (Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue) × 100

Frequently Asked Questions About Profit

What is a profit calculator?

A profit calculator estimates business profit by comparing total revenue with variable costs, fixed costs and other expenses.

How do you calculate business profit?

Business profit is generally calculated by subtracting total business costs from total revenue.

How is profit margin calculated?

Profit margin is commonly calculated by dividing net profit by total revenue and multiplying the result by 100.

What are break-even units?

Break-even units represent the estimated number of units that need to be sold for contribution from sales to cover fixed and other applicable costs.
