How to Calculate Business Profit

Business profit is generally calculated by subtracting total costs from total revenue. Revenue is the amount generated from sales, while total costs can include variable costs, fixed expenses and other business expenses.

The basic calculation is:

Net Profit = Total Revenue − Total Costs

This calculator uses the number of units sold, selling price per unit, variable cost per unit, fixed costs and other costs to estimate your business profit.

Profit Margin Calculator

Profit margin shows the percentage of revenue that remains after costs are deducted. A higher or lower profit margin can result from changes in selling prices, operating costs, variable costs or sales volume.

The calculator estimates profit margin using your net profit and total revenue.

Profit Margin = (Net Profit ÷ Total Revenue) × 100

Break-even Calculator

Break-even analysis estimates the number of units that need to be sold before total contribution covers fixed and other costs. It can help businesses understand the sales volume required to reach the break-even point.

The calculator estimates break-even units using the selling price per unit, variable cost per unit and fixed costs.

Revenue vs Profit

Revenue and profit are not the same. Revenue represents money generated from sales, while profit is the amount remaining after applicable costs have been deducted.

A business can generate substantial revenue while still producing a relatively small profit if its costs are high. Reviewing both revenue and profit therefore provides a clearer picture of business performance.
